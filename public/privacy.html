Privacy Policy

Slave Base · Last updated October 27, 2026

Slave Base is a private tool run by its owner to manage their own AI agents and business work. It is not offered to the public.

Google account access

When the owner connects Google Drive, Slave Base asks only for the drive.file permission. This lets it create a "Slave Base" folder and the documents and spreadsheets inside it, and update those same files. It cannot see, read or change any other file in the Google account.

The sign-in is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not shared with anyone and can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

What data is used

Slave Base copies work its agents produce (reports, notes, lists and PDFs) into the owner's Drive, and keeps business lists such as prospects and clients in the owner's own database. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not transferred to anyone else. Slave Base's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for Slave Base.
